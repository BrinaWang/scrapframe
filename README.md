# Scrapframe

A mixed media animation app for iPad and Apple Pencil, in a single web page.

Draw on every frame with 47 brushes in five sets (or your own imported PNG / Procreate brushes, and presets you save), on layers with blend modes,, import photos and video,
cut things out with scissors, add stickers, washi tape and torn paper, lay masking tape and peel it off,
pick a paper texture, and see neighbouring frames with onion skin.

Mixed media tools: photo effects (cyanotype, photocopy, halftone, duotone, riso, silhouette, glitch, transfer, weave),
scissors with star/circle/heart punches and cut-a-hole windows, torn and burnt edges, backing-paper and double borders,
text (handwriting, script, typewriter, stamp, ransom, label tape), lines and shapes, materials (croc leather, fishnet,
lace, gauze, vellum, wood, gold leaf), overlays (wax, grain, dust, folds, light leak, toner), pressed botanicals and
plaster flowers, and brushes for gesso, impasto, modeling paste, brayer, cyanotype, resin, gold and silver leaf. Export a frame as PNG, the animation as video,
or every frame as a PNG sequence.
